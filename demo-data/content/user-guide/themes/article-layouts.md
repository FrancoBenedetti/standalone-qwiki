# Article Layout Themes

Qwiki comes bundled with two primary layout themes designed for long-form content and documentation.

## Theme: Default (`theme-default.css`)
This is the standard, blank-slate theme. It relies entirely on the base `qwiki.css` file and is best if you want a clean, simple look without any opinionated styling.

## Theme: Modern (`theme-article-modern.css`)
The modern theme is designed for readability and visual impact.

**Features:**
* **Wider Layout**: The reading width is expanded for larger screens.
* **Bold Typography**: Headings are significantly larger and bolder.
* **Stylized Media**: Images automatically receive soft shadows and rounded corners.
* **Accented Quotes**: Blockquotes are styled with thick, colored left borders.

*Best for: Marketing pages, landing pages, and visually rich guides.*

## Theme: Classic (`theme-article-classic.css`)
The classic theme mimics the look of traditional academic papers or wikis.

**Features:**
* **Serif Fonts**: Uses classic serif typography (Merriweather/Georgia) for all content.
* **Underlined Links**: Ensures maximum accessibility for text links.
* **Dense Layout**: Slightly smaller text and tighter line-heights for information-dense pages.

*Best for: Technical specifications, legal documents, and academic writing.*
