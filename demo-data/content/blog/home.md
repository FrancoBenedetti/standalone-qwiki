# Welcome

My name is Franco Benedetti. For the past several years I’ve been collaborating on a project with a potentially monumental impact, and which we’ve dubbed the **Bizcloud Framework**. Jan Posthumus, my fellow collaborator in this venture, has with much thought and immense dedication compiled a description of what this is all about, so I won’t elaborate further here - but I urge you to follow the link below should you be wanting to know more and when you’re ready to look into it.

So why am I telling you this?

Well … we’ve now got to the stage where we have built a fully working technology demonstrator - and we are ready to commercialise the Bizcloud Framework. 

To do this, we require the right partner, and some capital. 

The search for the right partner has now started. Help us spread the word. Contact me at `franco+m@benedetti.co.za` for further information.

To find out more about our framework and its impact, bearing in mind that this is still a work in progress, please take a look at Bizcloud Framework.
